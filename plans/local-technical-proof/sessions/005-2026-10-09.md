API/CLI governance checks prove six floor-weakening rejections plus two floor-authority rejections and unchanged bindings. Expected metadata is never substituted for observed behavior.

5. **Split-segment privacy fixture:** A two-byte incomplete email fragment coincided with SQLite binary structure despite appearing in no decoded text/blob cell. Replaced only the two incomplete local/domain fragments with longer deterministic strings, preserving the same separate-segment rejection and Unicode code-point span. No scanner exemption or minimum-length bypass was added.
6. **Clean-shell setup and cache use:** Recursive proof/launcher processes now receive an explicit project import path. Pytest's assertion cache override is confined to the worker process; installed CLI/service children retain their prepared bytecode caches. Application deadlines were not enlarged.
7. **Deterministic concurrent update:** The former fixed two-second inference delay could expire before the binding update started. A separately authenticated fixed mock hold now proves arrival and continued in-flight state before and after the real generation receipt, then releases in a `finally` block. Its eight-second watchdog remains below the unchanged ten-second model deadline. Cold native-adapter initialization is warmed before this concurrency window; actual timing-fault scenarios retain their delays.
8. **Audit-writer lifecycle:** Real stalled-worker tests exposed a missed audit reserve. The single writer is prepared before readiness and retains an idle SQLite connection to avoid a last-connection WAL checkpoint on each request. Per-transaction connections, locking, absolute deadlines and FULL durability remain unchanged. Shutdown drains queued writes, closes the keeper on its owning thread and then closes the pool. Regressions cover WAL/readback, expired writes, checkpoint/integrity and real combined worker/SQLite contention.
